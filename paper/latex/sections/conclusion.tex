\section{Conclusion}
We presented \method, a retrieval-augmented framework for simultaneous speech translation that improves domain-specific terminology translation. \method couples a lightweight cross-modal retriever with a Speech LLM and is trained with noise-robust supervision so that it remains accurate under realistic retrieval errors at inference time. \crchange{Across all 12 ACL language--latency settings, \method improves exact-form terminology accuracy over InfiniSST by 12.3--21.4 percentage points; on the human-reference ESO En--De condition, the gains are 26.0--31.2 points. Form-aware scoring, end-to-end retrieval ablations, corrupted-retrieval tests, and qualitative analysis support the same terminology benefit. BLEU and XCOMET effects are smaller and mixed, so the evidence supports reliable glossary control rather than a universal improvement in general translation quality.}
